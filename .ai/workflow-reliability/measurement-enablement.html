1. Decision and boundary

Build producers, not another gate. Current collectors cannot supply complete mixed-language proof. Q1–Q5 enable all nine metrics; actual proof still gates release. Mutation stays 60%; evaluation stays 0.9.

New autonomous policy: 100% changed executable lines and decision outcomes under the declared models. This is not historical policy or human approval. An empty executable denominator is unavailable, not 100%. Tool qualification remains required.

3. Exact meaning of “measured”

Inventory includes tests/examples/benchmarks; coverage/mutation select changed production. Zero findings require successful processing. Unsupported inputs remain explicit. DirectoryRef = "." | Path permits root only in directory fields; file/artifact paths remain non-root, non-escaping.

Compare cycle/architecture identities in the literal-import graph; enforce layering and forbidden test/benchmark imports. Unresolved locals or parse failures are unavailable. Computed imports/reflection/commands remain outside this static model, not proved safe.

Producer validators bind raw records, exact receipt partitions, revision/source/tool/policy and owned artifacts; recompute values, denominators and verdict. Hash identity alone is not aggregate consistency, authenticated identity or containment.

4. Self-measurement without self-corruption

Retain the deterministic 20-candidate sample and expose omissions. Coverage selects tests, not mutants; uncertain mapping and survivors use full-suite confirmation. Python kills require the actual failed test assert instruction or qualified unittest failure path, not an ancestor assertion line. Setup/application errors and timeouts never kill.

The historical Python suite took about 12 minutes. Measure durations and cost the schedule before launch; keep the controller outside mutated target files. Budget exhaustion is incomplete, never tail dropping or timeout-as-survival.
