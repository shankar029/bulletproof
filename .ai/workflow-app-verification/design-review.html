4. Independence, feasibility and scope judgment

Does the plan massively overstate independence or verification? No in its stated claims; yes, its unqualified shared check exits would overstate per-slice completion if followed mechanically. It explicitly says sequential ownership, unconfirmed approval, combined design/planning context and NOT_RUN. Sequential increments need not be parallel or dependency-free to be independently verifiable. R1 repairs the actual problem: earlier slices currently reference later proof without a precise staged verdict. Phase 5 and Phase 6 still need actual independent observers; promised owners are not evidence they ran.

Size: I1 is a substantial walking skeleton spanning storage crash behavior, HTTP safety, UI, tests and tooling. Keep its vertical outcome, but order existing work into small internal steps if one implementer cannot maintain behavioral red/green cycles; do not introduce horizontal “completed” increments. The separate diagnosis CLI is acceptable only as a thin reusable replay entry over existing test helpers. Avoid a second expectation engine or a generic test framework. These are bounded design concerns, not additional blocking features.
